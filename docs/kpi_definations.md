# Workforce Analytics – KPI Definitions

## 1. Total Employees
Number of employee records in the dataset.

## 2. Attrition Count
Number of employees whose Attrition status is Yes.

## 3. Active Employees
Number of employees whose Attrition status is No.

## 4. Attrition Rate
Formula:

(Attrition Count / Total Employees) × 100

## 5. Retention Rate
Formula:

(Active Employees / Total Employees) × 100

## 6. Average Age
Average age of employees.

## 7. Average Monthly Salary
Average monthly income of employees.

## 8. Average Experience
Average total working years of employees.

## 9. Department Distribution
Number and percentage of employees in each department.

## 10. Job Satisfaction
Distribution of employee job satisfaction ratings.

## Business Success
The project should provide accurate workforce analysis, validated insights, clear visualizations and reproducible documentation.
